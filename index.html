Our Story

Where Ethiopian Tradition Meets Modern Craft

Founded in the heart of Addis Ababa, Roka Office and Household Furniture Manufacturing began as a small family workshop with a simple belief: that great woodworking honors both the material and the maker.

Today, we combine time-tested Ethiopian woodworking traditions with contemporary design and precision engineering. Every piece that leaves our workshop carries the soul of our craft — built by hand, finished with care, and made to endure.
